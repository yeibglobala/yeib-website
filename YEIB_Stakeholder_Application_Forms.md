WEBSITE CONTENT | DRAFT FOR REVIEW
Stakeholder Application Forms
Short-form content for the Nigeria YEIB Investment Funds website, covering the six stakeholder groups the Funds work with. Each form is designed to take about five minutes and to capture what is needed for initial screening only; full documentation is requested from applicants who proceed.
HOW THIS DOCUMENT IS ORGANISED

| Part 1 | Landing page copy and the stakeholder selector that routes each visitor to the right form |
| --- | --- |
| Part 2 | Section A: contact details, common to every form |
| Part 3 | Forms 1 to 6, one for each stakeholder group, with introductory copy and questions |
| Part 4 | Section C: declarations and consent, and the confirmation message |
| Part 5 | Drafting notes and points to confirm before the forms go live |

NOTES FOR THE WEB TEAM
Every visitor sees the landing page, then Section A, then the form for the group selected, then Section C.
Lines in green italics above a question are display conditions. A question is shown only when the condition is met.
Italic grey text under a question is help text to be shown beneath the field.
Word limits should be enforced in the field. Uploads should accept PDF only, with a 10 MB limit.
Each submission should generate a reference number in the format YEIB-[form number]-[year]-[sequence], for example YEIB-1-2026-0001.

## Form 1 | Entrepreneurs and businesses

| INTRODUCTORY COPY This form is for youth-led businesses with a working product or service, paying customers and a plan to grow. We invest in businesses that are majority owned or led by young people aged 18 to 35, or whose employees or customers are predominantly young people. We are sector agnostic. Our priority is job creation, so we look for businesses with the potential to scale and to create formal employment, including businesses operating outside the most concentrated markets. We pay particular attention to women-led businesses and to businesses with a positive environmental or climate impact. Depending on your stage, you may be considered for investment, for a capacity-building grant that pays for business development services from a vetted provider, or both. |

# Part 5 | Drafting notes and points to confirm
The following points should be resolved before the forms are published. All structural and regulatory positions reflected in the forms are provisional pending confirmation by legal counsel.
Youth definition. The Business Plan defines youth as ages 16 to 35; the Board version of the Project Appraisal Report uses 18 to 35 and a 51% youth ownership or management threshold. The forms follow the Project Appraisal Report. The age range should be confirmed and applied consistently across the website and the Investment Criteria and Exit Strategy.
Naira bands. Revenue and capital bands in Forms 1, 3 and 5 are derived from the US dollar ranges in the Business Plan (for example, target revenue of US$25,000 to US$1 million and capital sought of US$20,000 to US$500,000) at an indicative rate of about ₦1,500 to the dollar. The rate and the band boundaries should be confirmed, or the bands restated in US dollars.
No ticket sizes, allocations or targets published. The introductory copy describes focus only. Ticket sizes, the 65 / 10 / 25 allocation, gender and green deployment targets and the preferred return remain preliminary or subject to Board approval, and are therefore not stated on the forms.
Credit Guarantee Facility routing. It should be agreed with ICGL whether Form 3 submissions are captured on the YEIB website and passed to ICGL, or whether the form links directly to ICGL’s own onboarding for participating financial institutions. Declaration C.5 covers onward sharing in either case.
Investor form. Counsel should confirm whether hosting Form 6 on a public website is consistent with SEC rules on offers of interests in the Funds. Alternatives are a simple contact form, or making Form 6 available by invitation only. The disclaimer in the Form 6 introductory copy should be reviewed by counsel either way.
Contractual return question. Question 2.8 reflects the draft Investment Policy Statement position that not less than half of the indirect window goes to funds paying contractual yield from Year 1. If that position is not yet to be signalled publicly, the question can be removed without affecting the rest of Form 2.
Exclusion List and privacy notice. Declarations C.3 and C.4 link to a privacy notice compliant with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and to a public version of the Exclusion List (Annex to the Investment Policy Statement). Both need to be published before the forms go live, along with a data retention period for unsuccessful submissions.
Sector lists. The Funds are sector agnostic. Sector options are for classification and reporting only and should not be read as a list of priority sectors. The Form 1 introductory copy says this explicitly.
Placeholders. The response time in the confirmation message and the reporting email address for fraud are shown in square brackets for completion.
