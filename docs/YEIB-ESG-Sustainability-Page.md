# YEIB Investment Fund — ESG & Sustainability Page (Content Brief)

**Page:** ESG & Sustainability
**Source:** N-YIF Website Inputs doc
**Status:** Content requirements — ready for design/build

---

## Overview

The website should include a dedicated **ESG & Sustainability** section that clearly communicates the Fund's approach to responsible investment, environmental and social risk management, transparency, stakeholder engagement, and accountability.

The section has **four main sub-sections**, plus a related but distinct **Contact Us** section covering grievance redress, whistleblowing, and general enquiries.

---

## 1. Our Approach to Sustainability

Overview of how N-YIF integrates environmental, social, and governance (ESG) considerations across its investment activities and portfolio. Should cover, at a high level:

- **ESG Principles and Commitments** — N-YIF's overall commitment to responsible investment, environmental and social sustainability, good governance, and development impact.
- **ESG Governance and Accountability** — the governance structure for managing ESG matters, including oversight responsibilities and the roles of N-YIF, participating financial institutions, and other relevant parties.
- **Stakeholder Engagement** — N-YIF's approach to engaging beneficiaries, project-affected persons, communities, financial institutions, and other stakeholders throughout the investment lifecycle.
- **Impact and Performance** — an overview of key sustainability and development outcomes tracked by the Fund. Can progressively include indicators such as businesses supported, jobs created, youth beneficiaries, environmental performance, and portfolio E&S performance.

---

## 2. Reports & Disclosures

A central repository for N-YIF's sustainability-related reports and public disclosures. Documents may include:

- Annual Environmental and Social Reports
- Sustainability and impact reports
- Portfolio E&S performance reports
- Environmental and social assessments and management plans required to be publicly disclosed
- Stakeholder engagement-related disclosures
- Other sustainability or lender-required reports and disclosures

**Design requirement:** structure so additional reports can easily be uploaded and categorized over time (i.e., a scalable document library, not a static list).

---

## 3. Policies & Procedures

Access to relevant ESG policies, frameworks, and procedures governing N-YIF's activities. May include:

- Environmental and Social Management System (ESMS)
- Stakeholder Engagement Framework or Plan
- Grievance Redress Mechanism
- Vulnerable Persons or other applicable safeguards frameworks
- Other applicable ESG policies and procedures

**Note:** where a document isn't intended for full public disclosure, show a short description of the policy/framework instead of the full document.

---

## 4. Partnerships

Highlights N-YIF's partnerships and collaborations supporting its sustainability and development objectives. May include partnerships with:

- Development finance institutions
- Government institutions
- Participating financial institutions
- Development partners
- Technical assistance providers
- Other organisations supporting the Fund's ESG, sustainability, and development objectives

**Content requirement:** briefly explain the relevance of each partnership to the Fund — **not just a logo wall.**

---

## Contact Us

Three separate, clearly identifiable channels — kept visually and functionally distinct from each other:

### A. Grievance Redress

A dedicated Grievance Redress Mechanism enabling project-affected persons, beneficiaries, communities, and other stakeholders to raise environmental, social, or other project-related concerns.

The page should provide:
- Purpose of the grievance mechanism
- Who can submit a grievance
- Available submission channels
- Key stages of the grievance handling process
- Indicative acknowledgement and response timelines
- Confidentiality information
- Escalation options if a complainant is dissatisfied with the resolution
- Confirmation that raising a grievance will **not** result in retaliation or adversely affect access to N-YIF programmes or financing

**Build requirement:** an accessible **online grievance submission form**, plus email/telephone/other channels.

### B. Whistleblowing

A separate channel for reporting suspected fraud, corruption, misconduct, unethical behaviour, conflicts of interest, or other breaches.

The page should clearly explain:
- What matters may be reported
- Available reporting channels
- Confidentiality arrangements
- Whether anonymous reporting is permitted
- Non-retaliation protections
- How reports are escalated and managed

**Important:** Grievance Redress and Whistleblowing must remain clearly differentiated — they serve different purposes and should not be merged into one form or one page section.

### C. General Enquiries

A separate channel for enquiries that are **not** grievances or whistleblowing reports.

---

## Data Privacy

Because grievance and whistleblowing channels may collect personal or sensitive information, the site needs a **Privacy Notice / Data Protection Statement** explaining how submitted personal information is collected, stored, used, and protected.

---

## General Website Requirements (apply across all ESG content)

- Clearly visible and easy to locate from the main website navigation
- Written in accessible, plain language
- Mobile-friendly
- Structured to allow additional reports, policies, and disclosures to be added over time
- Accessible to persons with disabilities where practicable
- Supported by clear document dates and version information, so stakeholders can identify the most current policies and disclosures
